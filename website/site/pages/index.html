Features

One assistant that sees the whole picture of your work.

Meeting tools only hear your calls. Search tools only see your files. Whimbrel captures both, and everything it does downstream gets better because of it.

Meeting notes without a bot

Recording starts from your calendar or when Whimbrel hears a call begin. You get a transcript with speakers, notes blended with your own, and decisions and action items when the meeting ends.

How meeting capture works →

Screen memory, as text

Whimbrel reads the text of the window you are working in and builds a timeline of everything you saw and wrote. Find that number, that thread, that doc, without remembering where it was.

How screen memory works →

Ask your work history

"What did we decide about pricing with Acme?" Chat answers from your own meetings, screen history, and email, and cites the moment each answer came from.

Routines that brief you

Scheduled routines prepare you before each meeting, recap your day at the end of it, protect focus time on your calendar, and summarize your week.

See routines →

Connected to your tools

Gmail, Google Calendar, and Outlook 365 connect in a click. Whimbrel is built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and other AI tools can query your Whimbrel memory through its local MCP server.

One timeline, every device

Use Whimbrel on your work laptop and your desktop, Mac or Windows. Each device adds to one end-to-end encrypted timeline, labelled with where it happened.

How it works

Install it once. Then just work.

  1. Install and grant access

    A guided setup walks you through each permission Whimbrel needs, such as microphone and screen text, and explains why.

  2. Work the way you do now

    Whimbrel captures meetings and screen text in the background. Pause it from the menu bar or tray whenever you like.

  3. Ask, brief, follow up

    Search your history, ask questions with citations, and let routines draft the recap and the follow-up email.

Privacy by architecture

Your memory belongs on your machine.

An assistant that remembers everything has to be one you can trust with everything. Whimbrel is built so that trust does not depend on a promise.

  • Stored locallyMeetings, transcripts, and screen history live in a database on your own computer.
  • End-to-end encrypted syncDevice keys never leave your devices. The sync service stores only ciphertext.
  • You choose where AI runsKeep everything on-device with Ollama, or route chosen work to the hosted model you pick.
  • Exclusions and retentionExclude apps from capture and set how long history is kept.
  • Open sourceApache 2.0, with no telemetry. Read how it works, or run your own sync relay.
