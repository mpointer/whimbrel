For people who work in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Codex

Give Claude your whole workday, not only your meetings.

Your AI tools start every conversation knowing nothing about your work. Whimbrel remembers your meetings and what was on your screen, on your own computer, and lets the tools you choose ask it.

Download for Mac Download for Windows
  • Local MCP server, read-only
  • You grant each tool its own access
  • Free and open source

Meeting notetakers hand your AI a transcript. That is one hour of your day.

The rest of your work happened in documents, tickets, threads and browser tabs. Whimbrel captures that too, as text, and makes it something your tools can search.

Meetings, without a bot

Transcripts with speaker names, summaries, decisions and action items, recorded from your computer's own audio and transcribed on your machine.

What was on your screen

The text of the windows you worked in, kept as a searchable timeline. Text, not screenshots, so it stays light and easy to exclude from.

One question, all of it

A connected tool can ask for the context behind a question and get the matching meeting lines, screen history and recent work together.

What a connected tool can ask.

Seven read-only tools over a local MCP server. Each client gets a token with only the scopes you choose.

Search your memory

search_memory and assemble_context: keyword and meaning search across screen history and meeting speech, ranked together.

Read your meetings

list_meetings, get_meeting and get_transcript: summaries with decisions and action items, and the full transcript when you grant it.

Reconstruct a day

get_timeline and get_daily_digest: what you worked on, in order, for "what was I doing on Tuesday?"

Or as plain files.

Whimbrel can keep an Obsidian vault up to date: a note for each customer and project, its people, organizations and meetings, with your own notes preserved. Point Claude Code, Codex or any agent that reads a folder at it.

Your memory is not on our servers.

The memory is a database on your computer and the MCP server answers from it locally. There is no hosted copy for a tool, or for us, to read. Run Whimbrel's own summaries and answers on a local model, or on your own API key.
