For consultants, lawyers, advisers and recruiters

When a client asks what you hold on them.

Every client page in Whimbrel has four controls: delete the client, keep their material for a set time, export it, and exclude it from hosted AI models. They are on Mac and Windows.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Four controls on a client's page.

Delete this client

Delete the client and what Whimbrel holds on them in one step on current devices, with a receipt of what was removed and what you still need to check. Some derived copies stay on your other devices; see below. You see the list first and type the client's name to confirm.

Keep for a set time

Choose how long to keep a client's material: 3, 6, 12, 24 or 36 months, or until you delete it. When items reach that age, Whimbrel shows you the list and deletes only after you confirm.

Export

Write one client's meetings, email, calendar items, conversations, steps and page to a folder you choose, in plain files you can read without Whimbrel.

Exclude from hosted models

Material that names or involves the client is kept out of requests to hosted AI models. It applies from the moment Whimbrel files it under the client, and new material is checked locally first.

The receipt.

After a delete, Whimbrel shows counts by category: meetings, emails, calendar events, steps and so on. It also lists what you still need to check yourself, under Left for you. The receipt holds no client name and no client text. On a Mac you can save it as a file.

Your other devices

The delete goes to your other devices at once, and each current device applies it when it syncs. The receipt shows how many still need to update before they apply it. Phones apply deletes but do not start them.

No list of deleted clients

Whimbrel keeps no record of a deleted client, not even the name. If new email or a new meeting later matches, the client can reappear as a new topic.

Derived material

On the device you delete from, summaries, suggestions, people and organisations that Whimbrel made and that name the client are removed with it. On your other devices, routine results, profile suggestions, people's names and meeting briefs that named the client can stay: the delete does not remove them there.

What exclusion can and cannot do.

Whimbrel recognises a client's material by matching their name, aliases and known attendee emails on your computer. No model is involved, and the check runs before any hosted request about new material. A call that never names the client, with attendees Whimbrel does not know, cannot be recognised, so the claim covers material that names or involves the client.

Matching is on whole words. A name inside another word, or in text written without spaces (some inflected forms, some scripts), may not match, and that material can still go to a hosted model.

The setting syncs to your other devices once they have been updated. While a device that has not been updated is still on your sync, the setting stays on the device where you set it, and the toggle says so.

A question that names the client, or is asked from the client's page, is answered by a local model or not at all. Material that already left cannot be recalled.
