For consultants, lawyers, advisers and recruiters

Your client calls stay on your computer.

Whimbrel records your meetings without a bot, writes the notes, and keeps a page for every client. Recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your Mac or PC, not on our servers. AI runs on your machine, or on a model you choose.

Most meeting notetakers upload the call.

The recording goes to the company's cloud to be transcribed, and the transcript stays there. For a client conversation, that is one more party holding it. Whimbrel does the work where the call happened.

Transcribed on your machine

Whimbrel turns speech into text on your own computer, with speaker names. The audio is never uploaded to be transcribed.

Stored with you

Meetings, transcripts, notes and your work history live in a database on your computer. We have no copy and no way to read one.

No bot in the call

Nothing joins your meeting. Whimbrel listens to your computer's own audio on Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack huddles and in-person conversations.

AI where you decide

Run a model on your own computer and no text goes to a model provider. Or choose a hosted model for better answers, knowing the text of each request goes to it.

Nothing hidden

On the Mac, Settings → Your data shows what Whimbrel keeps, every way data can leave your computer, and whether each one is switched on.

Yours to take or delete

Export everything to a folder you can read without Whimbrel. Forget a meeting and it is removed from every device you own.

A page for every client.

Name your clients and matters. Whimbrel files your meetings, email and calendar under them, on your computer, and keeps one page for each.

What you owe, and what you are owed

Steps from meeting action items and email, each quoted from where it came from, with the date it was asked and when it is due.

Ready before the call

A short brief before each meeting: where things stand with this client, what was decided last time, and what is still open.

Ask your own history

"What did we agree on fees with Acme?" Whimbrel answers from your meetings and email and cites the line each answer came from.
