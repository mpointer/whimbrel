Phone app beta

Try Whimbrel on your phone

The phone app shows the meetings Whimbrel records on your Mac or Windows PC. Read the notes, decisions and action items wherever you are.

  • You need Whimbrel on a Mac or Windows PC. The phone doesn't record meetings itself.
  • No computer yet? You can still ask. We'll keep your request.
  • We approve testers one at a time and email you when your spot is ready.
  • iPhone: through Apple's TestFlight. Android: a direct download.
