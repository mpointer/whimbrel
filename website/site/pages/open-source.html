Open source

An AI assistant you can read, run, and keep.

An assistant that remembers your work should be one you can inspect. Whimbrel is open source under the Apache 2.0 license, every feature is free, and nothing about it requires our servers.

What's open

All of it: the Mac and Windows apps, the shared core that does capture, transcription, memory, search and chat, the routines and connectors, the MCP server, the sync relay, and the update feed. There is no "community edition" with features held back for a paid one.

The public repository opens with the first release under the Whimbrel name. This page will link to it then.

Your models, your bill

Run models on your own computer with Ollama, or bring a key for Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google Gemini and pay that provider directly. Whimbrel has no hosted model and never resells AI usage, on any plan. Teams will be able to hand one organization key to every seat; it's still the organization's own key.

Run your own relay

Sync between your devices is end-to-end encrypted, and it goes through a small relay: one Cloudflare Worker, plus a Turso database per account. You can use the relay we host, which syncs one computer and one phone for free, or run your own in your Cloudflare account, with no device limit. The free tiers of Cloudflare and Turso are enough for personal use. The self-hosting guide ships in the repository.

What we'll charge for

Only things that need a server somebody runs, or a person on the other end:

  • Cloud (planned): our relay for any number of devices, daily backups, push notifications to your phone, and routines that keep running while your laptop is closed.
  • Team (planned): shared meeting memory with consent from whoever captured it, routines that hand work to a teammate, and one bill.
  • Support: a person responsible for your self-hosted deployment.

Nothing is for sale yet. See pricing for what's planned.

Our commitments

  • Anything that runs on your device stays in the free, open app.
  • We never resell or mark up AI usage.
  • The app ships no telemetry.
