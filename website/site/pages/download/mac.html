System requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or laterIncluding macOS 15 Sequoia and newer.
  • Apple siliconM1, M2, M3, M4 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • Disk spaceRoom for the app, the on-device speech model, and your growing history.
  • Optional: OllamaTo run language models fully on your Mac.

Install in two minutes

  1. Open the downloaded Whimbrel-x.y.z.zip. macOS unzips it to Whimbrel.app.
  2. Drag Whimbrel.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Whimbrel. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.
  4. Follow the setup to grant microphone, audio, and Accessibility access.
  5. Connect your calendar so recordings start with your meetings.
