Whimbrel vs. Bluedot

Bluedot is invisible to the other people on your call. That is not the same as private from the vendor: its recordings are stored in its cloud, and Whimbrel's are stored on your computer.

Every Bluedot claim links to the page it came from, read on November 11, 2026. "Not found" means we did not find it stated on the pages we read. Products change; check Bluedot's site for current details. About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • You want recordings stored on your computer, with no cloud copy to lose when you stop paying.
  • You want unlimited meetings and history on the free app.
  • You want a local model, your own key, and open source.

Choose Bluedot if…

  • You record in the browser or on your phone or watch, including in-person meetings.
  • You need CRM sync, SSO and SCIM, or a SOC 2 Type II report.
  • You want a flat company-wide price.
