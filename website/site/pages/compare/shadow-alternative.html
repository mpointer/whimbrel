Whimbrel vs. Shadow

The closest twin: both capture meetings without a bot and keep recordings on your computer. Shadow is a narrower Mac app with hotkey actions. Whimbrel adds Windows, a memory of the work between meetings, and a page per client.

"Not found" means we did not find it stated on the pages we read. Products change; check Shadow's site for current details. About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • You use Windows, or want an iPhone or Android app that reads your notes.
  • You want a memory of the work between meetings and a page for every client.
  • You want to use a local model or your own key, and read the source.

Choose Shadow if…

  • You want to press a shortcut on any screen to draft a reply, ask about the window, or dictate.
  • You want the simplest possible Mac app with meetings as plain Markdown files for Obsidian or Claude Code.
  • AI features at $8 a month suit you better than Whimbrel's options.
