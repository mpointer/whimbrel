Whimbrel fits if…

  • Your work history shouldn't live in someone else's cloud.
  • You want to choose the AI model, including fully local ones.
  • You want memory of the whole workday, not just your meetings.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need shared team workspaces or SSO today (Granola, Littlebird).
  • You need to record meetings from your phone (Granola).
  • You depend on a long list of ready-made integrations (Littlebird).
