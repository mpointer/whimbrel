Whimbrel vs. Fellow

Fellow sells privacy as controls for an organisation: consent records, retention, redaction and audit. Whimbrel's answer is architecture: recordings kept on your own computer. They suit different buyers.

About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • You are your own buyer, with confidentiality duties and no compliance department.
  • You want recordings kept on your computer rather than controlled in a vendor's cloud.
  • You want to choose the model that sees your text.

Choose Fellow if…

  • A compliance team needs consent records, retention rules, redaction and an audit trail.
  • You need SSO, a SOC 2 Type II report or a HIPAA agreement.
  • You want agendas and 1:1s as well as notes, across a team.
