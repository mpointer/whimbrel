Littlebird vs. Granola

Choose Granola if you mainly want meeting notes your team can share, with strong phone apps. Choose Littlebird if you want an assistant that also remembers your work outside meetings and connects to many of your tools. Both keep your history in their cloud. If you want it on your own computer, Whimbrel is a third option.

Choose Granola if…

  • Meetings are what you want help with, and a free plan with unlimited notes and 30 days of history is enough.
  • Your team shares notes and uses Slack, HubSpot, Attio or Notion.
  • You want apps on iPhone, Android and Apple Watch.

Choose Littlebird if…

  • You want answers about everything you worked on, not only your meetings.
  • You want email, calendar, Drive and Slack connected, even on the free plan.
  • You want to ask questions by text message from your phone.

A third option: Whimbrel

Whimbrel takes meeting notes without a bot, like both, and remembers your screen, like Littlebird. The difference is where it all lives: recordings, transcripts and history stay on your computer, not on Whimbrel's servers. You can run AI with a local model or your own key, and the app is open source. It is free, with optional paid plans.

It is not the right pick if you need team workspaces or SSO today, or want to record meetings from your phone. See Whimbrel vs. Littlebird, Whimbrel vs. Granola, or the three-way comparison.
