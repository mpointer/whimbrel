Whimbrel vs. Granola

Both take meeting notes without a bot in the call. The difference is what happens outside your meetings and where your data goes. Also see the three-way comparison with Littlebird.

Every Granola claim links to the page it came from, read on November 11, 2026. "Not found" means we did not find it stated on the pages we read. Products change; check Granola's site for current details. About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • You want answers that draw on meetings and the documents, email and apps around them.
  • Meeting audio and notes should be stored on your computer, not in a vendor cloud.
  • You want to run local models, or choose your own AI provider.

Choose Granola if…

  • Your team shares and works on meeting notes every day.
  • You need to capture meetings from your phone or watch.
  • You need SSO, an audit API, or a vendor SOC 2 Type II report.
