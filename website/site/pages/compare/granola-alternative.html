Whimbrel vs. Granola

Both take great meeting notes without a bot in the call. The difference is everything that happens outside your meetings, and where your data goes. Also see the three-way comparison with Littlebird.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • You want answers that draw on meetings and the documents, email, and apps around them.
  • Meeting audio shouldn't leave your computer to be transcribed.
  • You want to run local models, or choose your own AI provider.
  • You'd like briefings and recaps prepared on a schedule.

Choose Granola if…

  • Your team shares and collaborates on meeting notes every day.
  • You need to capture meetings from your phone.
  • You only want meeting notes, nothing else.
