Whimbrel vs. Littlebird

Both remember what you work on and take meeting notes without a bot. The difference is where that memory lives: Littlebird's is in its cloud, Whimbrel's is on your computer. See also the three-way comparison with Granola.

Products change; check Littlebird's site for current details. About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • Your client calls and work history should be stored on your own computer, not in a vendor cloud.
  • You want to run a local model, or choose the provider that sees your text.
  • You want a page per client with what is owed, and the source open to read.

Choose Littlebird if…

  • You want your email, calendar, Slack and Drive connected with no setup, including on the free plan.
  • You need SOC 2 Type II, a HIPAA agreement or SSO from the vendor.
  • You want to ask questions from your phone or by text message.
