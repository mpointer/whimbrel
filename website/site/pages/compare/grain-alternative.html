Whimbrel vs. Grain

Grain is built for a team's shared library of calls. Whimbrel is built for one person's whole memory, stored on their own computer.

About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • You work alone or in a small practice and want your own memory, not a shared library.
  • Recordings and history should be stored on your computer.
  • You do not need CRM write-back, clips or coaching.

Choose Grain if…

  • A sales or customer success team wants a shared call library with CRM sync.
  • You need SSO, SCIM, audit logs and a SOC 2 Type II report.
  • You want free viewer seats for colleagues who only watch.
