Whimbrel vs. Voicenotes

Voicenotes leads with "confidential meetings". Its privacy policy says recordings are stored on AWS servers in the United States. Whimbrel stores recordings on your computer.

Voicenotes' home page says "Nothing joins your call, nothing trains on your data, every word stays." Its privacy policy (effective October 12, 2026) is the source for where data is stored. Every Voicenotes claim links to the page it came from, read on November 11, 2026. "Not found" means we did not find it stated on the pages we read. Products change; check Voicenotes's site for current details. About Whimbrel: recordings, transcripts and history are stored on your computer and not on Whimbrel's servers. AI runs on your computer or on a model you choose; with a hosted model, the text of each request goes to that model. Every way data can leave is listed in the app.

Choose Whimbrel if…

  • Your client's call should not be stored on someone else's servers.
  • You want to choose whether any text goes to a hosted model, and see each request that leaves your computer.
  • You want a page per client and a memory of work between meetings.

Choose Voicenotes if…

  • You record on your phone or watch every day, and want dictation everywhere.
  • You want ChatGPT and Claude connectors that work from the web and your phone.
  • You need a vendor SOC 2 report or recording-consent controls on an Enterprise plan.
