Languages

Your meetings, in your language.

Whimbrel works the way you do, whether your day runs in English, Spanish, French, German, or Portuguese. Or all of them.

The app in your language

Spanish, French for Canada and France, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and US, British, Canadian, and Australian English.

Notes in the language you choose

Summaries, action items, and Ask answers can use a different language from the app, so a meeting in one language can give you notes in another.

Meetings in other languages

On Mac, Whimbrel detects the language of each meeting and transcribes it as spoken, on your own computer.

Search across languages

Search understands more than 50 languages. Ask in Spanish and find what was said in English.

Slides in their own language

Text on slides and shared screens is read in the language it was written in, so it stays searchable.

Still private

Transcription and search run on your computer. Language support doesn't change where your data lives.
