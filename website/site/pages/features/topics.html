Topics

Everything about a customer, on one page.

Name the customers, products, and projects you care about. Whimbrel files your email, meetings, calendar, and screen activity under them, and keeps a page for each one.

Before the call, you know where things stand.

A topic page answers one question first: do I owe anything before this meeting?

The short version on top

One line with the next key date or step and the next meeting. For example: "SOW due Fri, Nov 14 · next call Tue, Nov 18, 10:00".

What you owe, and what's owed to you

Steps from meeting action items, email, and Teams chats, each quoted from where it came from, with the date it was asked and the due date when one was given.

Your role

Whimbrel works out whether you lead, support, or are kept informed. Correct it once and it learns from you.

The next meeting

Date, time, and who is attending, counted by company, so you can see who is coming from each side.

Recent activity

The latest emails, meetings, and screens you had open, such as a Salesforce task or a project document, newest first.

One click from anywhere

Open a topic from a meeting on Today, a meeting's notes or prep brief, a search that names it, or your next meeting in the menu bar or tray.

Steps you can trust.

A list of stale or wrong to-dos is worse than none, so Whimbrel shows its work and lets you correct it.

Check off, note, dismiss

Check a step off, add a note such as "sent v2 Monday", edit it, or dismiss it as not yours. Meeting action items stay in step with the meeting's own list.

Closed by what came next

When a later email says it's done, the step moves to Done with a note saying what closed it. You can reopen it.

Old steps step aside

A step with no activity on its topic for 60 days moves to an Older list instead of cluttering the page.

In your Obsidian vault

Whimbrel can keep an Obsidian vault up to date. Turn it on in Settings → Your data (Mac) or Settings → Capture & Privacy (Windows). Each topic's page, its people, organizations and meetings are written as notes. Your own notes are kept, and the vault stays on your computer.

On your phone

The iPhone app has a Topics tab with each topic's page, built by Whimbrel on your computer and synced end-to-end encrypted. It is read-only for now.

Local first. Your choice after that.

Your local model sorts items into topics and finds steps on your own computer. With your own TypeSafe key you can switch either job to TypeSafe for better accuracy. It sends short excerpts with secrets removed, shows a cost estimate, and starts only when you press Start.
