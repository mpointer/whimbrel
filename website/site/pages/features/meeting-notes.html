Every meeting, captured the same way.

Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Slack huddles, a phone call on speaker, a conversation at your desk. If your computer can hear it, Whimbrel can take notes on it.

Starts on its own

Recording begins from your Google or Outlook calendar, or when Whimbrel detects meeting audio for a call that wasn't on the calendar.

Transcribed on-device

A Whisper speech model runs locally. Audio doesn't leave your computer to be transcribed.

Your notes, improved

Type quick notes during the call. Whimbrel blends them with the transcript into clean notes with decisions and action items.

Prepared before you join

Meeting prep pulls your last conversations and open questions with the same people into a brief before the meeting starts.

Ask about any meeting

"What did they say about the timeline?" Answers cite the exact moment in the transcript.

Follow-ups drafted

Whimbrel can draft the follow-up email in Gmail for you to review and send.
