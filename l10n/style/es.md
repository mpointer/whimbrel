# Spanish (es; ships as es-US)

- Neutral Latin American Spanish that reads naturally in the US and Mexico. Address the user as tú, never usted or vos.
- Keep it as short and plain as the English. UI labels are short; sentences end with a period as in the source.
- Use ¿…? and ¡…! where Spanish needs them.
- Keep every placeholder (%@, %lld, %1$@, {name}) exactly as written, in a natural position for Spanish.
- Product and feature names stay in English: Whimbrel, Ask, Away Mode, Today, Recap, Routines, skin.
- Skin voice lines keep the skin's register: Almanac stays old-fashioned, Retro Terminal stays terse.
- Dates and numbers are formatted by the OS; do not write them out.
